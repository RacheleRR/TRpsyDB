About TRpsyDB
TRpsyDB is a curated, evidence-tiered database of tandem repeat loci with relevance to neuropsychiatric disorders — schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, autism spectrum disorder, major depressive disorder, and ADHD.
Unlike general TR resources (STRipy, STRchive, WebSTR), TRpsyDB focuses specifically on the psychiatric TR landscape — including candidate and uncertain loci — and provides integrated annotation against SCHEMA, BipEx, and GWAS gene sets, plus an interactive analysis tool powered by the TRex pipeline.
